# Climon AI SEO Audit

A live AI-powered SEO audit and landing page optimization tool for startups and small businesses.

## Live Prototype

https://audit.climon.se/Landing

## About

This project helps businesses analyze their websites, identify SEO opportunities, improve landing page performance, and create better conversion-focused marketing strategies.

## Focus Areas

- AI-assisted SEO audits
- Landing page optimization
- Conversion rate optimization
- Website performance analysis
- Marketing automation
- Small business growth

## Why I Am Building This

Many small businesses struggle with expensive marketing services and limited access to advanced AI tools. This project aims to make SEO and digital marketing insights more accessible through AI and automation.
